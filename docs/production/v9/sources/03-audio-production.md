# CHRONOS: NOX & LULÙ\
## MASTER AUDIO & MUSIC PROMPT PACK — PREMIUM ANDROID

### PROMPT 00 — MASTER SONIC DIRECTION

Create a completely original premium audio identity for **CHRONOS: Nox & Lulù**, a cinematic cyber-fantasy action-adventure set in Vanta, a decaying city where industrial infrastructure, forgotten technology, human communities and impossible temporal machinery coexist.

The soundtrack must stop feeling like a small collection of interchangeable loops. Every district, major mission, boss, emotional confrontation and gameplay state must have its own recognizable sonic identity while remaining part of the same musical universe.

Core musical DNA: the recurring **“Two Knocks” motif** — two short repeated notes followed by a response. It represents Nox awakening, Lulù answering, recognition, consent, separation and eventual reconnection. Transform this motif rather than repeating it literally.

Nox's musical identity: low register, restrained analog synths, processed electric bass, metallic percussion, irregular pauses, imperfect mechanical pulses, physical weight, suppressed emotion.

Lulù's musical identity: warmer upper register, glass-like synths, resonant mallets, delicate arpeggios, breathing pads, subtle human rhythmic imperfections, protective rather than sentimental character.

When Nox and Lulù are together, their motifs can interlock. When separated, deliberately leave harmonic or rhythmic space where the other motif would normally answer.

World identity: industrial cyber-fantasy rather than generic cyberpunk. Combine degraded electronics, mechanical resonances, intimate orchestral textures, prepared percussion, distant bells, metal, glass, air, electrical hums and restrained synthetic sound design.

Avoid generic trailer music, EDM drops, heroic Hollywood brass, cheerful chiptune, generic synthwave and obvious imitation of existing games or films.

All music must be **instrumental**, loop-friendly where required, with clear midrange space for dialogue, weapon telegraphs and mobile speakers. Strong character even at low playback volume. Original composition only.

# MUSIC — TITLE, CHARACTERS AND GLOBAL STATES

### PROMPT 01 — MAIN TITLE / “DUE COLPI”

Compose a 75–90 second main-title theme for CHRONOS: Nox & Lulù.

Open almost in silence: distant electrical infrastructure, soft room resonance, then **two physical metallic knocks**. After the second knock, introduce the core melodic answer.

Begin intimate and mysterious, suggesting a person waking in a place that should have remained sealed for almost two centuries. Gradually reveal the scale of Vanta with low analog synth, restrained strings, distant industrial percussion and fractured bell harmonics.

Around the midpoint, let the Nox and Lulù motifs briefly answer one another without fully resolving. End with a beautiful but incomplete chord and one distant mechanical clock resonance.

Emotion: mystery, identity, affection, danger waiting beneath ordinary city life.

No vocals. No triumphant ending. Seamless enough for title-menu continuation.

### PROMPT 02 — NOX EXPLORATION THEME

Create a 90-second seamless exploration theme for Nox.

92–98 BPM. Dark but not hopeless. Low analog bass, muted mechanical percussion, processed metallic taps, sparse synth lead and occasional glass harmonic.

The “Two Knocks” motif appears as two low notes followed by a descending response.

Nox should sound physically capable but internally uncertain. Use deliberate gaps in the arrangement to suggest missing memories and self-control rather than aggression.

The music must support long exploration without becoming tiring.

### PROMPT 03 — LULÙ EXPLORATION THEME

Create a 90-second seamless exploration theme for Lulù.

100–106 BPM. Warmer and more mobile than Nox's theme. Resonant mallets, light modular arpeggios, soft bass pulse, airy pads and subtle acoustic textures.

Transform the Two Knocks motif into two delicate repeated tones followed by an ascending answer.

The music should communicate intelligence, care and determination without making Lulù sound fragile or naïve.
