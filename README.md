# City Adventure

Production, visual and audio packs.

## Current playable baseline

- Blackout v0.6 remains the verified Android/WebView playtest baseline.
- Nox and Lulù are the central protagonists.
- Four on-foot districts and three Vortex driving missions remain canonical.
- The Officina/Rifugio Blackout decision is persistent and is the model for future consequence design.

## Commercial target

**Premium paid Android game — €4.99.**

Production assumptions:
- complete paid experience;
- no forced ads;
- no energy timers;
- no pay-to-win;
- offline-capable main campaign;
- release blocked until the premium quality gates are met.
